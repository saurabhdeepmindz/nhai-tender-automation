💬 Frequently Asked Questions
View All FAQs →
How do I register as a vendor on the platform? +
To register as a vendor, visit the NHAI website and navigate to the registration section. Fill in your company details, upload required documents (PAN, GST, registration certificate), and submit. You will receive login credentials via email within 24-48 hours after verification.
How long does it take to receive a response to my query? +
The AI system generates initial responses within seconds. Admin review and approval typically takes 4-6 hours during business hours. For complex queries, it may take up to 24 hours. You will receive email notifications when your query is answered.
Can I edit or delete a query after submission? +
You can edit or delete queries only if they are in "Pending" status and have not been reviewed by the admin. Once a query is under review or answered, you cannot modify it. However, you can submit a new query for additional clarifications.
How does the AI generate responses to queries? +
The AI system uses Natural Language Processing (NLP) to understand your query, searches through the RFP document and historical query database, and generates responses with exact clause and page references. All AI-generated responses are reviewed and approved by NHAI administrators before being published.
What file formats are supported for document uploads? +
The system supports PDF, DOCX (Word), JPG, and PNG formats. Maximum file size is 10MB per document. For technical drawings, we recommend using PDF format for better quality.
How do I download prebid response documents? +
Navigate to your Query History page, look for published prebid response documents, and click the download button. Documents are available in both PDF and DOCX formats. You will also receive email notifications with download links when new responses are published.
