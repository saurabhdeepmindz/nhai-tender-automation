🔍 Identified Issues & Recommendations

Missing Geotechnical Investigation Data
📌 Section: Technical Specifications 📄 Page: 10-12
Critical
The RFP does not include geotechnical investigation reports for structure locations. As per SBD template clause 4.2.1, detailed soil investigation data must be provided to bidders for foundation design and cost estimation.
💡 Recommendation:
Include comprehensive geotechnical reports as annexure or issue corrigendum with soil investigation data for all structure locations (bridges, culverts, retaining walls).
EMD Amount Not Specified
📌 Section: Clause 2.4 📄 Page: 15
Critical
The Earnest Money Deposit (EMD) amount is not clearly specified in Clause 2.4. SBD template requires explicit mention of EMD value and exemption criteria.
💡 Recommendation:
Specify exact EMD amount (e.g., ₹2.5 Crores) and clearly state exemption criteria for MSME/startups as per government guidelines.
Conflicting Specifications - RE Wall vs TCS
📌 Section: Schedule B, Clause 11.1 & 2.10 📄 Page: 28, 45
Major
Clause 11.1 (Page 45) specifies RE wall for chainage 0+020 to 0+680, but Clause 2.10 (Page 28) proposes TCS 3A for location 0.120 to 0.980. Specification conflict for location 0.020 to 0.120.
💡 Recommendation:
Issue corrigendum clarifying which specification applies for chainage 0.020 to 0.120. Ensure Schedule B and technical specifications are aligned.
Payment Terms - Missing Milestone Details
📌 Section: Clause 8.2 📄 Page: 34
Major
Payment terms mention monthly progress payments but specific milestone percentages and completion criteria are not clearly defined. SBD requires detailed payment schedule.
💡 Recommendation:
Add detailed payment schedule with milestone percentages (e.g., 15% on completion of earthwork, 25% on pavement layers, etc.).
Ambiguous Clause Reference Numbering
📌 Section: Multiple Sections 📄 Page: Various
Minor
Some clauses use different numbering systems (e.g., 2.4.1 vs 2.4(a)), making cross-referencing difficult. SBD recommends consistent hierarchical numbering.
💡 Recommendation:
Standardize clause numbering system throughout the document following SBD template format (e.g., 1.1, 1.1.1, 1.1.2).
Missing Definitions for Technical Abbreviations
📌 Section: Throughout Document 📄 Page: Various
Minor
Several technical abbreviations (TCS, RE, BHS, RHS, LHS) are used without definitions in glossary. SBD requires comprehensive definitions section.
💡 Recommendation:
Add definitions section or expand existing glossary to include all technical abbreviations used in the RFP.
