NATIONAL HIGHWAYS AUTHORITY OF INDIA
Request for Proposal (RFP)
Construction of four lane Arrah bypass (Asni to Baonpali)
from Km 0+000 (Design Chainage) to Km 4+500 (Design Chainage)
of NH-319 (Old NH-30) in the State of Bihar on EPC Mode

1. Introduction

The National Highways Authority of India (NHAI) invites bids from eligible contractors for the construction of a four-lane bypass on National Highway 319 in Bihar. This project encompasses the development of approximately 4.5 kilometers of highway infrastructure on an Engineering, Procurement, and Construction (EPC) basis.

2. Project Overview

The scope of work includes earthwork, pavement construction, drainage systems, retaining structures, safety measures, and all associated civil works as detailed in the technical specifications and drawings provided as part of this tender document.

3. Eligibility Criteria

Bidders must meet the following minimum requirements to be considered eligible for this project:

  • Minimum average annual turnover of ₹50 Crores in the last 3 financial years (2022-23, 2023-24, 2024-25)
  • Experience in completion of similar highway projects of not less than ₹40 Crores in the last 7 years
  • Valid registration and licenses as per applicable regulations
  • Technical and financial capability as per Section 3 of this RFP
Admin Note - Jan 10, 2026
Clarification added via Corrigendum-II: Both domestic and international projects are included in turnover calculation.

4. Technical Specifications

All work shall be executed in accordance with the Ministry of Road Transport and Highways (MoRT&H) specifications and IRC codes. Specific technical requirements are detailed in Schedule B of this document.

The contractor shall ensure quality control measures are implemented throughout the project duration, with regular testing and certification as per the quality assurance plan.

5. Payment Terms

Payments shall be made on a monthly basis based on the progress of work certified by the Engineer. The payment schedule includes milestone-based disbursements with retention as specified in Clause 8.2 of the contract agreement.
